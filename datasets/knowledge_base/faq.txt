=================================================
ORDER & SHIPPING FAQ
=================================================

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Customers can track orders from the Track Order section.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days.

Q: What if my package is delayed?
A: Eligible customers may receive a compensation voucher.

Q: Can I change my shipping address?
A: Address changes are allowed before shipment.

Q: What happens if my package is marked delivered but I did not receive it?
A: Raise a Missing Item complaint for investigation.

=================================================
RETURNS & REPLACEMENTS FAQ
=================================================

Q: Can I return a laptop after 15 days?
A: No. Laptops have a 7-day return window.

Q: Can I return accessories?
A: Accessories can be returned within 15 days.

Q: Do I need the original packaging?
A: Yes. Original packaging is preferred for returns and replacements.

Q: What if I receive a damaged product?
A: Submit a complaint with evidence for replacement or refund evaluation.

Q: What if I receive a wrong product?
A: You are eligible for a replacement if verified.

=================================================
REFUND FAQ
=================================================

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days.

Q: How can I check my refund status?
A: Customers can view refund status in their account.

Q: Can I request a refund after the return window expires?
A: No, unless approved as a special exception.

Q: Will shipping charges be refunded?
A: Eligible refunds include applicable order charges according to policy.

=================================================
PAYMENT FAQ
=================================================

Q: Which payment methods are supported?
A: UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Wallet and Net Banking.

Q: Payment was deducted but the order was not created.
A: Raise a billing issue complaint for investigation.

Q: I was charged twice for the same order.
A: Submit a billing complaint and provide transaction details.

=================================================
ACCOUNT FAQ
=================================================

Q: How do I change my phone number?
A: Update it from the account settings section.

Q: How do I update my email address?
A: Verify your identity before changing email.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the Forgot Password option on the login page.

=================================================
WARRANTY FAQ
=================================================

Q: My product stopped working during the warranty period.
A: Submit a warranty claim for review.

Q: Does warranty cover accidental damage?
A: No. Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.

Q: What documents are required for warranty claims?
A: Order details, invoice and supporting evidence.

=================================================
VOUCHER FAQ
=================================================

Q: When is a compensation voucher issued?
A: For eligible delivery delays and service issues.

Q: How long is a voucher valid?
A: Vouchers are valid for 90 days.

Q: Can multiple vouchers be combined?
A: Voucher usage depends on promotional terms and conditions.
